## 21 October 2026: Meet Spark ✨

**Spark introduces you to someone a step ahead of you.** Every day,
Meetwell finds a member with more experience in your field (or one close to it) and tells
you why you two should talk. One tap and you're saying hello.

**How it works**
- Turn it on in **Dashboard → Spark** and tell us your field, years of experience, what
  you're looking for and your city. Only Spark sees these. They never show on your card.
- Each morning you may get one introduction by email, with a short note on why you were
  matched and a "Say hello" button that opens their card.
- Spark only suggests people with **4+ years of experience** and **a profile photo**, who
  have more experience than you.
- Introductions go one way. Only the person getting the introduction is told, so nobody gets
  a flood of requests.
- No monthly limit. At most one introduction a day, and never the same person twice.
- Matches are ranked by field, extra experience, interest in mentoring and city. When the
  Claude key is added, Claude (Anthropic's AI model) picks the best fit and writes the reason.

**Who can use it**
- Members with a confirmed account and a card that's at least **80% complete**.
- Under 80%? You'll get a one-time nudge to finish your card. Experienced members without a
  photo hear that people want to learn from them, and a photo is what puts them on the list.

**The Spark email**
- A personal note from Rasheed, with the person's photo (or their initials), name and role.
- A "Why we matched you" box and a "Say hello to {name} →" button.

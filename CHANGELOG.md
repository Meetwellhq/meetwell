# Meetwell changelog

What's new in Meetwell, newest first. Live at [meetwell.me](https://meetwell.me).

---

## 24 October 2026: Event pages

- Partners like ThinkUX now get a page for each event, with every speaker's card in one place.
- Each event has a QR code for slides and posters, and each speaker gets a QR code for their badge.
- Scan, save a speaker's card, or message them straight from the event page.

## 23 October 2026: A new look on your phone

- A menu button at the top right of your phone, with everything in one place: Overview, your card, QR code, Contacts, Messages and Settings.
- Mentors and partners find Mentoring and their Partner page there too.

## 23 October 2026: Meetwell on your home screen

- Add Meetwell to your phone's home screen and open it like an app.
- Your card is one tap away, with no app store needed.

## 22 October 2026: Contacts

- Everyone whose card you scan or open is kept in your new Contacts page, with notes and search.
- Save one person to your phone, or export everyone at once.
- See who saved your card, and save them back.
- Scanned a card but don't have an account? The person's card is your invite to join.

## 22 October 2026: Message a mentor, and Spark credits

- Members with 4+ years can become mentors on Meetwell and set their own monthly price.
- Members earlier in their careers can pay for 30 days of private messages with a mentor. No reply in 7 days? Full refund.
- Mentors keep 80% and withdraw to their bank.
- Want more Spark introductions? Buy credits on your Spark page.

---

## 21 October 2026: Spark for every stage of your career

- **Under 4 years:** Spark introduces you to experienced people a step ahead of you, up to **5 free a month**.
- **4 years or more:** Spark recommends you to people earlier in their careers, and your Spark page shows
  how many were introduced to you.
- Your years of experience and field stay private and never show on your card.

---

## 21 October 2026: Meet Spark ✨

**Spark introduces you to someone a step ahead of you.** Meetwell finds a member with more experience
in your field (or one close to it) and tells you why you two should talk. One tap and you're saying hello.

- Turn it on in **Dashboard → Spark** and tell us your field, experience, what you're looking for and
  your city. Only Spark sees these.
- You get at most one introduction a day by email, with a short note on why you were matched.
- Introductions go one way, so nobody gets a flood of requests, and never the same person twice.

---

## 18 October 2026: Private beta

- **Early access:** join the waiting list, and invited people sign up with a personal access code.
- QR codes now carry the Meetwell logo in the middle.
- Every card has a "Try it for free" bar so visitors can make their own.

## 17 October 2026: Meetwell is born

- Your networking card with its own QR code. People scan it with their phone camera. No app needed.
- One-tap **save contact** to their phone.
- **Ready-made first messages** the other person can edit and send.
- Email, WhatsApp, website and social links in one tidy row.
